Privacy Policy

User privacy is paramount. Falsetto operates under the strict principles of data minimization and compliant EU data handling.

What data do we collect?

  • Anonymous Players: We do not collect, track, or save any personal identifier tokens. You play entirely anonymously.
  • Registered Users: If you choose to sign up, we save only your chosen username, your high scores, and your cryptographically hashed and salted credentials. Your actual password is never visible to us.

Data Hosting & Security

All game databases and credentials are saved securely on self-hosted infrastructure located within the Czech Republic (European Union). Standard modern encryption protocols are utilized to safeguard account entries.

Cookies & Tracking

We host this application without any third-party analytics tracking tools (such as Google Analytics) or advertising cookies.

Age Restrictions & Child Privacy

In accordance with GDPR and COPPA regulations, Falsetto does not knowingly collect or solicit data from individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 without parental guidance in relevant EU zones). If you are under the required age, please enjoy the game anonymously without registering an account.

Your Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR)

You retain full ownership and rights over your account data. You can instantly wipe your account data permanently at any moment through your Account Settings panel, or request a manual database wipe by reaching out via our Contact Page. Once initiated, your profile configurations and score histories are permanently removed from our databases.

Falsetto is powered by open metadata engines and developer APIs. We do not host, sell, or claim ownership over any music tracks, metadata, or lyric segments rendered during gameplay.
